## Answers

**Character limit:** The goal is meant to be one short line, about 15 words or fewer. Your full mission is 30 words, so your shorter version fits better.

**What the goal does:** It guides my future suggestions, like what to build or improve next and which results to report. It is not an analytics setting, and it doesn't change your site or what gets tracked.

**One headline number:** The goal holds only one headline number. These are the options I can track reliably:
- **Resources purchased each month:** paid guidebook and Resource Library purchases. Each one is confirmed by Stripe and recorded once, so this is the most reliable number. It counts revenue directly.
- **Resources downloaded each month:** the current number. It measures educational reach but not sustainability.
- **Newsletter signups confirmed each month:** counts only people who click the confirmation link. It measures community, not revenue.

Amazon book sales happen on Amazon, so the site can't count them. I can count clicks to Amazon, but a click isn't a sale.

**Recommendation:** Resources purchased each month. It matches your sustainable-revenue priority and is the most trustworthy number. Downloads, newsletter signups, community referrals, visitors and engagement stay as separate measures that I report alongside it, so the educational mission stays visible.
