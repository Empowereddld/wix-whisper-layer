# Website audit: health check + user experience ideas

A full, report-only audit of empowereddld.com. Nothing gets changed — you get a written report of what's broken, what's weak, and ideas to improve, including the path from landing to purchase.

## What gets checked

### 1. Broken things
- Crawl the live site: every page in the menu, footer, and buttons — flag any page that fails to load, shows an error, or links to a dead address
- Check every page at phone, tablet, and desktop widths for cut-off content, overlapping elements, or sideways scrolling
- Watch for behind-the-scenes errors (failed image/script loads, console errors) on each page
- Verify the recent fixes hold up: the song popup on all screen sizes, the header at laptop widths, the /music page

### 2. Speed and SEO health
- Re-run the built-in SEO foundations scan and compare with what's already on record
- Check page titles, descriptions, sitemap, and search-engine instructions are consistent
- Spot-check how the site appears in search data (Semrush estimates) — which pages get found, and obvious gaps
- Flag anything slowing pages down (oversized images, heavy scripts)

### 3. User experience review
- First impressions: what a new visitor sees in the first seconds (announcement bar, popup, hero) and whether too many things compete at once
- Navigation: can a parent, educator, or therapist find their path quickly; is the mobile menu tidy
- Sign-up flows: Resource Library and Story Pros — any confusing steps or dead ends
- Readability and tone consistency across pages

### 4. Sales journey
- Trace the path a visitor takes from the home page to buying something (books, merch, resources)
- Check the shop and product pages: are products easy to find, do images load, is pricing clear
- Identify where interested visitors likely drop off before purchasing
- Ideas to improve: better product visibility, clearer calls to action, featuring bestsellers

## What you get

A written report organized as:
- **Broken** — things that don't work and should be fixed
- **Needs attention** — things that work but hurt the experience
- **Ideas** — improvements worth considering, ranked by likely impact

Each item includes what it is, where it is, and what I'd suggest doing about it.

## Out of scope
- No changes to the site, emails, or backend — report only
- No changes to the announcement bar wording, EmailOctopus setup, or consent flow
- Visitor numbers come from estimates and your click counter; no new tracking is added
